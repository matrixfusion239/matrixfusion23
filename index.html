About

Most businesses aren't short on data — they're short on clarity. I build the systems that turn raw numbers into decisions.

Matrix Fusion works with manufacturing SMEs, e-commerce brands, and growing B2B businesses that are sitting on more data than they can use. My job is to close that gap — with Power BI dashboards, Excel systems, business reports, AI automation workflows, and AI chatbots that take routine work off your plate.

The goal is always the same: real-time visibility into how the business is performing, and less time spent building reports by hand.
